Data Processing Addendum (DPA)

Last Updated: 8 May 2026

Version: v1.0

1. Scope

This DPA applies where Metiflow Group Ltd processes personal data on behalf of business customers using TraderFlow.

2. Roles

Customer acts as data controller for customer-submitted personal data. Metiflow Group Ltd acts as processor for that data, except where acting as controller for account administration and legal compliance.

3. Processor Obligations

  • Process personal data only on documented customer instructions.
  • Implement industry-standard safeguards and commercially reasonable security measures.
  • Restrict personnel access based on need-to-know principles.
  • Assist with data subject rights and incident response where required.

4. Subprocessors

We use subprocessors to deliver hosting and platform services. The current list is maintained at /subprocessors.
