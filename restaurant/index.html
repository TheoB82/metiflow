Why WiFi-only hardware?

Devices with mobile data can silently switch networks mid-service, losing sync with the relay. WiFi-only models can't switch — they stay on your network or they stay offline, so you always know the state.

What if a device disconnects?

An 8-second grace period covers brief WiFi blips. After that, the screen shows a red offline banner and key actions (Send to Kitchen, Mark Done) are disabled until the device reconnects.
