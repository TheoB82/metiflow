All features available on every plan

📋

Professional Quotes

Create quotes fast with company defaults. Searchable customer lookup. Convert approved quotes straight to jobs. Full project context end-to-end.

✅

Full Job Lifecycle

Jobs flow through planning → ready → scheduled → in-progress → completed. Assign team members, manage readiness checklists, track status in real-time.

💰

Auto-Invoice & KPIs

Invoices generate automatically on job completion. Track revenue by customer and period. Monitor job counts, conversion rates, and performance.

📅

Calendar & Team Scheduling

Week/month/day views with drag-and-drop scheduling. Unscheduled jobs as external cards. See team availability and job details in one view.

🛠️

Materials & Purchase Orders

Track materials, unit pricing, and suppliers. POs with full status workflow from draft to delivered. Attachments and approval workflows included.

⚡

RAMS & Risk Management

Attach RAMS templates to jobs. Question types: yes/no, text, checklists, risk matrices. Calculate risk scores automatically and route through approvals.

🧾

Expense Tracking

Log site expenses, upload receipt photos, route through approval. Full job cost analysis with complete visibility from pending to approved.

👥

Team & Permissions

Multi-organisation support with owner/admin/member roles. Granular permissions per feature. Scales from solo to 10 team members.

📸

Job Documentation

Attach photos by type (before/after/site condition). Time tracking via job sessions. All visual and operational context tied to the job.
