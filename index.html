Metiflow is the parent brand. TraderFlow is the first child product, focused on trade businesses that need one place to run quoting, work delivery, and commercial follow-through without the usual admin drag.

TraderFlow

The first Metiflow product is built around how trade teams actually work.

It covers the full management loop from the first quote through job completion and invoicing, with scheduling, materials, RAMS, and team coordination built into the same flow.

Commercial flow

Quotes, customers, invoices, and recurring work visibility.

Operational flow

Jobs, calendar scheduling, team assignments, and live status tracking.

Site readiness

Materials, purchase orders, expenses, and RAMS approvals in one place.

Management view

KPIs, dashboards, role-based access, and multi-organisation support.
