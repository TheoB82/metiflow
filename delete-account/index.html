Metiflow Resto

How to request deletion

To request deletion of your Metiflow Resto account and associated data, email admin@metiflow.com from the email address registered to your account, with the subject line "Account deletion request". Include your venue name so we can locate your account.

We'll confirm your request and complete deletion within 30 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your login account and profile (name, email, authentication credentials).
  • Venue configuration data: menu, tables, staff records, settings.
  • Diner contact details (name/phone) collected for bookings or takeaway orders.
  • Device pairing and sync records associated with your venue.

What may be retained, and why

Order, payment and financial transaction records may be retained after account deletion where required for accounting, tax, or other legal obligations (typically up to 6 years in the UK). This data is retained in a restricted, non-account-linked state and is not used for any other purpose.
