One device on your network runs as the master relay. Every tablet, phone, and Mac connects to it — orders, table status, and kitchen tickets are synced instantly across all screens.

🗺️

Table Management

Visual floor plan with live table status. Enable or disable table service per venue. QR code generation for each table. Seat assignments and course tracking per table.

🍳

Kitchen Display System

Dedicated station views — food only, bar only, or all. Course tabs (Starters, Mains, Desserts) always visible. Bump tickets as they're prepared. Timing on every item.

📱

Order Taking

Full menu with categories, modifiers, and course assignment. Works on any Android or Mac. Drafts save automatically. Send to kitchen with one tap — syncs instantly across all devices.

🛵

Takeaway & Pre-orders

Schedule takeaway orders for later. Delivery and collection types. Upcoming orders feed into kitchen display when it's time to prepare — not before. ASAP and timed orders.

📅

Bookings

Reservation management with date, time, party size, and guest notes. View upcoming bookings at a glance. Integrates with table availability. No third-party booking platform needed.

💳

Bill & Payments

Itemised bill screen with cash and card payment recording. Tip tracking. Split payments across a table. Bill status syncs to all devices instantly. Order auto-closes when settled.

📊

Daily Takings

Owner-only revenue dashboard. Today, this week, and this month at a glance. Breakdown by cash and card. Tips tracked separately. Always up to date as payments come in.

📡

Live Relay Sync

One device on your network is the truth. All others connect to it over WiFi. If a staff device loses connection, their Send and Mark Done buttons disable automatically until they're back online.

🔒

Role-based Access

Owner, waiter, kitchen, bar — each device sees only what it needs. Table editing is owner-only. Menu management, venue config, and reset service are locked to admin roles.
