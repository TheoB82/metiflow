What TraderFlow covers today

Everything you need to run a trade business smoothly

Live & available now
📝
Live

Professional Quotes

Create quotes fast with company defaults (VAT, validity periods). Searchable customer lookup. Convert approved quotes straight to jobs. Track customer and project context end-to-end.

✅
Live

Full Job Lifecycle

Jobs flow through planning → ready → scheduled → in-progress ⇄ paused → completed. Assign team members, manage job readiness checklists, track status in real-time with full team visibility.

💰
Live

Auto-Invoice & Business KPIs

Invoices generate automatically on job completion. Track revenue by customer and period. Monitor job counts, conversion rates, and performance. Financial year-aware period filtering for accurate reporting.

📅
Live

Calendar & Team Scheduling

FullCalendar week/month/day views with drag-and-drop scheduling. Unscheduled jobs appear as external cards. See team availability, location context, and job details in one view. Seamless crew coordination.

🛠️
Live

Materials & Purchase Orders

Track material qty, unit pricing, and suppliers. Create purchase orders with status workflow (draft→sent→confirmed→delivered→cancelled). Upload attachments and approval workflows. Keep site inputs aligned with job scope.

⚡
Live

RAMS & Risk Management

Attach RAMS templates to jobs. Question types: yes/no, text, checklists, and risk matrices. Capture answers, calculate risk scores automatically. Route through approval workflows without leaving the job.

💳
Live

Expense Tracking & Receipts

Log site expenses, upload receipt photos, and route through approval. Track status: pending → receipt uploaded → approved or rejected. Build accurate job cost analysis with full visibility.

👥
Live

Team & Permissions

Multi-organization support with owner/admin/member roles. Granular permissions: quotes, jobs, invoices, materials, calendar, RAMS, suppliers. Scales from solo to unlimited teams on Pro tier.

📸
Live

Job Documentation

Attach photos to jobs, mark type (before/after/site condition). Time tracking via job sessions. Keep all visual and operational context tied to the job. Team notes and collaboration built in.
